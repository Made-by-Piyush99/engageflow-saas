How to Get Your Meta & Instagram API Keys

Follow these official Meta Developers steps to connect your Instagram account. It takes about 3 minutes.

1

Create a Meta Developer Account & App

1. Open developers.facebook.com and sign in with your Facebook account.

2. Click on "My Apps" in the top navigation bar, then click "Create App".

3. Choose "Other" as your use case, and select "Business" as your app type.

4. Give your app a name (e.g. "My Instagram Auto DM") and click Create App.

2

Add Instagram Graph API to Your App

1. On your app dashboard, scroll down to "Add products to your app".

2. Locate "Instagram Graph API" and click "Set Up".

3. Ensure your Instagram account is an Instagram Professional (Creator or Business) Account connected to a Facebook Page.

3

Generate Page Access Token & Account ID

1. In the top menu, go to Tools → Graph API Explorer.

2. Select your App from the top-left dropdown.

3. Under Permissions, add these essential scopes:

instagram_basic instagram_manage_comments instagram_manage_messages pages_show_list pages_read_engagement

4. Click "Generate Access Token" and approve the Facebook login dialog.

5. In the query box, run: GET /v21.0/me/accounts?fields=instagram_business_account{id,username} to get your Instagram Business Account ID.

4

Configure Webhook in Meta

Under your App settings → Webhooks → Instagram, enter:

Callback URL: https://YOUR_DOMAIN/api/webhooks/instagram
Verify Token: EngageFlow_public_webhook_2026

Subscribe to fields: comments and messages.
