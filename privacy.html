Privacy Policy for EngageFlow

Effective Date: November 26, 2026 | App ID: 2401678503569810

EngageFlow (operating as "IG Auto-DM Pro", "we", "us", or "our") is dedicated to protecting your privacy and ensuring transparency regarding how we handle information obtained through Meta Platforms, Inc. (Instagram Graph API).

1. Information We Collect

When you authorize EngageFlow with your Instagram Business or Creator account, we access data strictly via official Meta Graph API permissions:

  • Basic Account Details: Instagram username, Account ID, business profile image, and follower count.
  • Post & Media Information: Identifiers, captions, timestamps, and permalinks of your Instagram Reels, Posts, and active Stories.
  • Inbound Public Comments: Comment text, username of the commenter, and timestamp for the purpose of matching your configured automation keywords.

2. How We Use Collected Data

We use your information exclusively to provide the core services of EngageFlow:

  • Detecting keyword triggers (e.g. "LINK", "PRICE", "CODE") in comments on your designated media.
  • Dispatching direct message (DM) responses and delivering requested resources or links to your followers.
  • Posting automated public comment replies and likes on your behalf as configured in your automation rules.
  • Displaying conversion analytics, DM delivery volume, and engagement metrics inside your private dashboard.

3. Data Sharing & Security

We do not sell, rent, monetize, or trade your personal information or follower data with any third-party advertisers. All communication with Meta Graph API is encrypted using industry-standard TLS/SSL (HTTPS).

4. User Data Retention & Deletion

You maintain complete control over your data at all times:

  • You can revoke our app's access anytime via your Instagram settings (Settings > Apps and Websites > Active > Remove).
  • You can request immediate and permanent deletion of all stored data by visiting our Data Deletion Instructions Page or emailing us directly.
