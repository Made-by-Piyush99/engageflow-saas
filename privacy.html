Privacy Policy for EngageFlow

Last updated: November 2026

EngageFlow ("we", "our", or "us") is dedicated to protecting your personal information and your right to privacy.

1. Information We Collect

We receive basic account information through Meta Graph API (Instagram / Facebook Login) including your username, profile picture, account ID, and media information necessary to automate direct messages and comment replies.

2. How We Use Information

Data collected is strictly used to process comment auto-replies and dispatch direct messages according to automation rules configured by you.

3. Data Deletion

Users can disconnect their Instagram account at any time from the EngageFlow dashboard, which immediately revokes stored tokens and deletes associated cached data.

4. Contact Us

If you have any questions, contact us through the app dashboard.
